PETIFY PROJECT IDEA

1. BRAND VISION AND EXPERIENCE
Petify is a premium, nature-connected destination for exotic pet enthusiasts. The
experience should help keepers discover animals and the care essentials they need,
while making responsible, informed pet care central to the brand.

Use a clean, modern visual identity built around electric deep blue and crisp white,
with emerald green, aqua teal, and amber gold accents. Bring in subtle organic and
aquatic inspiration, gentle glass-like surfaces, clear typography, and purposeful
micro-interactions.

2. CATALOG AND PRODUCT ROADMAP
Available-now catalog:
- Exotic fish, marine life, and selected small mammals or reptiles.
- Species-specific foods, bio-diets, and nutrition.
- Enclosures, cages, terrariums, and aquariums.

Coming soon:
- Smart habitat accessories, including misting and heating systems, lighting,
  enrichment, and habitat decor. Keep these clearly distinct from products that
  are currently available.

Future platform capabilities:
- Recommend compatible food and habitat products when a keeper selects a species.
- Capture interest in upcoming products with email sign-up and species preferences.
- Add an interactive enclosure configurator and a care and feeding tracker.

IMPLEMENTATION NOTE
Keep the storefront, catalog data, and future keeper tools modular so the experience
can grow without confusing available inventory with planned products.
